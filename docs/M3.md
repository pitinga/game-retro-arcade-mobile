# M3 — FREE catalogue candidate
G01 Canyon Wings and G02 Horizon Rally are independent geometric prototypes added to G03 FREE. No final art, copied ROM, billing or ads. See GDD-GAMES.md; this candidate does not replace normative requirements.
- G01: continuous linear-interpolated corridor, max center change 36u per H/3, width 46–60% W (initial 56%), cells at center ≤0.73H interval, seeded spawns, drag/boost/brake/fire, energy, collision/lives, sector checkpoint with no duplicated score. Beacons are two optional paths within a wide corridor; full branched route topology and space-time BFS with moving enemies not yet validated. Respawn restores energy cells without repeated points. Energy resets at sector transition as provisional balance choice; verify against GDD in review. 
- G02: 90s day + 3s countdown, accelerating/braking and drag/steer, 2.5D road, time-of-day, fog/ice, rivals, one-time overtakes, integrity/goal, daily bonus. Initial generation reserves one visible safe lane and uses fixed rival lanes (no lane-change behaviour yet); traffic speed 45–100 km/h subset of GDD range, selected provisionally for reference agent margin. Weather overlay is prototype; visibility test on device remains pending.
- Tests: 1000 structural seeds for corridor/cell intervals and lane reservation; representative reference agent for twelve day-12 scenarios; deterministic replay 30/60/120 over ten seconds; selected AC regressions and FREE routing. Do not claim 1000 full playthroughs or solver proof, 3-minute replay, final balance, full AC acceptance, accessibility or mobile performance.
- CI renders two portrait aspect ratios, game and pause for each M3 module; count/colour validation. Screenshots are desktop Linux, not Android.
- M2 still has physical device and independent visual review gates; M3 draft can be developed but must not merge until gates are satisfied. RA-001 similarity and legal clearance remain required before final art. M4 not started.
